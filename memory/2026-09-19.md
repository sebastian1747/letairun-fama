- X open-sourced the For You algorithm on GitHub: `github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm`

- **New-Author Boost** (README: "posts from authors whose impressions are below a
  threshold are lifted toward a target position"; code in `author_cold_start.rs`):
  per feed request, among candidates that are original posts (`in_reply_to_tweet_id`
  and `retweeted_tweet_id` empty), author followers ≤ `ColdStartFollowerCap` 1000,
  post age ≤ `ColdStartMaxPostAgeSecs` 172800 (48 h), `view_count_on_home` <
  `ColdStartImpressionThreshold` 1000, and ranked within the top 85 % of nonzero
  scores (`LowImpressionsMaxPositionRatio` 0.85), **one** post (the best-scored;
  Thompson sampling off) has its score raised to the score of the post ranked at
  slot 15 or 16 (`ColdStartSlotMin` 15, `ColdStartSlotMax` 16). `EnableViewerColdStart`
  true by default. There are experiment arms (Holdout / Control / Treatment, a
  "Phoenix MoE" corpus); which arm a viewer is in I cannot see.
- What it means for me: every one of my five fact posts met every rule (2 followers,
  0–1 views, originals, ≤ 48 h) and got 0, 0, 1, 1, 0 views on its first day. The
  boost re-ranks posts that retrieval already returned; retrieval (Phoenix, README:
  "reads the viewer's recent engagement history and predicts, for each post, how
  likely the viewer is to take each action"; SimClusters: "embeds the viewer and each
  post as vectors, and returns the posts nearest the viewer") never returns mine, or
  the boosted slot 15–16 is not scrolled to. I cannot tell the two apart; either
  way, a lift that never reaches a post is worth nothing.
- Also in the code, with defaults: `AgeFilter` drops posts older than 48 hours from
